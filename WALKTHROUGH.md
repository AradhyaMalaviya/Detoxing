# Product Walkthrough Script — DetoxWithBagga

## 1. Introduction (0:00 – 1:00)
"Welcome to DetoxWithBagga—an enterprise-grade digital emotional wellness platform built using Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Lucide Icons. Designed with an empathetic, calming dark aesthetic adapted directly from our master brand standards, DetoxWithBagga offers a safe, non-clinical space for individuals navigating overthinking, stress, relationship confusion, and burnout."

## 2. Product Tour (1:00 – 4:00)

### Hero & Brand Identity
"Notice the immediate visual harmony. Our hero section communicates emotional safety, featuring 2 complimentary sessions badges, social proof counters (500+ conversations, 10,000+ community members), and our reading recommendation pill badge ('Reading: Your Mind Matters 💌')."

### Audience & Topic Exploration
"Visitors can interact with tailored audience cards (Students, Young Professionals, Relationships, Personal Growth, Emotional Wellness) and explore 16 core discussion topics with instant category filtering and detail preview modals."

"The animated timeline outlines our 12-hour response SLA. Our session request section provides real-time client validation and bridges directly to the official Google Form."

### Safety, Non-Therapy Disclosure & Crisis Support
"We clearly distinguish non-clinical support from therapy, presenting international crisis hotline cards for visitors requiring immediate medical attention."

### Educational Hub & Instagram Integration
"Our educational resources hub offers essays with modal reading experiences and actionable exercises. Our Instagram section faithfully replicates the brand profile from our reference image."

## 3. Engineering Highlights (4:00 – 6:00)
1. **Next.js 15 App Router & Turbopack**: 100% static prerendering success with sub-second page generation.
2. **WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility**: Full keyboard support, skip links, contrast ratios > 4.5:1, and reduced motion overrides.
3. **Structured Data SEO**: Full JSON-LD schemas (`FAQPage`, `EducationalOrganization`) and OpenGraph metadata.
4. **Design System Flexibility**: CSS custom variables with seamless Dark/Light theme switching.
5. **Clean Component Architecture**: Modular TypeScript components with zero placeholders or dead code.
