# Google Form Configuration & Workflow Guide — DetoxWithBagga

## Overview

The DetoxWithBagga platform integrates both an inline interactive request form and a direct link to the official Google Form (`https://forms.gle/dtXs8yhF937H4RREA`) as seen on `@detoxwithbagga` Instagram profile (`30590.jpg`).

---

## Form Field Mapping

| Website Field | Google Form Question | Type | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full Name | "What is your preferred name?" | Short Text | Yes |
| Age | "How old are you?" | Short Text | No |
| Email Address | "Your email address for session scheduling" | Email | Yes |
| Instagram Handle | "Instagram Username (optional)" | Short Text | No |
| Country & Timezone | "Country and Timezone" | Short Text | Yes |
| Preferred Language | "Preferred language for discussion" | Dropdown | Yes |
| Selected Topics | "Which topics would you like to focus on?" | Checkboxes | Yes |
| Situation Description | "Share a brief summary of what is on your mind" | Paragraph | Yes |
| Preferred Duration | "Session duration (30, 45, 60 mins)" | Multiple Choice | Yes |
| Time Slot | "Preferred time slot (Morning, Evening, etc.)" | Multiple Choice | Yes |
| Consent & Safety Check | "Acknowledge non-therapy disclaimer" | Checkbox | Yes |

---

## Response Management & SLA

1. Enable **"Get email notifications for new responses"** in Google Forms Responses tab.
2. Maintain a **12-hour SLA response guarantee** by reviewing incoming responses twice daily.
3. Reply to the applicant using official email (`support@detoxwithbagga.com`) or Instagram DM with 2–3 proposed session time slots.
